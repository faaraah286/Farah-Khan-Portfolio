Chapter 01 • The Narrative

How Research Rigor Meets Real Capital Decisions

A career shaped by reading thousands of institutional filings—and then stepping inside operating businesses to put capital to work.

Act I
S&P Global Market Intelligence • Equity & Banking Research

Learning How Global Markets Read Numbers

My career began at S&P Global Market Intelligence, working closely on institutional equity and banking research across Asian and European markets. Every day, I examined financial statements, capital ratios, and corporate filings that global portfolio managers relied on to make investment decisions. That experience taught me a simple habit that still guides my work today: never take headline numbers at face value—always check the footnotes and cash flows underneath.

Act II
E-Commerce & Abu Dhabi Healthcare • Capital Allocation

Making Capital Decisions Where Every Dirham Counts

After building a strong foundation in public market research, I stepped inside operating businesses—first in e-commerce, and then in Abu Dhabi’s healthcare sector, where I worked directly with a founder on real capital allocation decisions. Before launch, we compared two competing startup options—a retail pharmacy versus a café—by modeling upfront costs, licensing rules, and expected returns to decide where limited capital would be safest and most productive.

Once we chose the pharmacy, local Abu Dhabi rules meant operating strictly on cash during the first year before qualifying for insurance networks. I modeled footfall, competitor pricing, and medication margins to pick a location that could break even on cash sales alone, and later structured supplier credit terms to free up working capital. As Year 2 insurance eligibility approached, I evaluated local insurance plans—weighing reimbursement discounts and payout delays against patient demand—so the business only signed contracts that genuinely added value.

Act III
CFA Charter & MBA Finance • GCC Deal Craft

Bringing Research Discipline and Operating Judgment Together

Today, backed by an MBA in Finance and the CFA Charter, I combine both worlds—the disciplined valuation methods of an equity research analyst and the practical judgment of someone who has seen how businesses actually make, protect, and reinvest cash in the UAE.
